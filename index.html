A teacher's journey, turned into a system for hundreds of teachers.

Smija K S founded MathsManthra Academy and Manthra Designs after seeing how many talented teachers, tutors and abacus trainers were capped by the four walls of a classroom, no matter how good their teaching was.

Since then, she has guided over 500 educators through the shift from classroom-only teaching to confident, income-generating online academies, combining practical teaching experience with AI tools, personal branding and digital marketing systems built specifically for educators.

Her Mission

To help teachers move beyond traditional classrooms and confidently build successful online education businesses — with a clear system, not guesswork.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Everything teachers usually ask before joining the workshop.

Can beginners join?

Yes. The workshop is designed for teachers at every stage — whether you're just curious about online teaching or already tutoring and ready to scale.

Do I need technical knowledge?

No prior tech experience is needed. Every tool and system is taught step-by-step, in plain language, live during the sessions.

Is there lifetime support?

You'll join a growing community of MathsManthra teachers, with ongoing guidance from our team after the workshop ends.

Can I teach from home?

Absolutely — the entire system is built for teachers running their academy from home, including many who started as homemakers.

How do I get students?

The Student Acquisition System, taught in the workshop, gives you a repeatable process for attracting and enrolling students consistently.
